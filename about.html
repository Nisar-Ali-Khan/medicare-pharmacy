Our Story

Trusted Healthcare, Delivered to Your Door

MediCare Pharmacy was founded in 2018 with a simple mission: to make genuine, affordable medicines accessible to everyone in Pakistan. We started as a small pharmacy in Islamabad and have grown into one of the most trusted online pharmaceutical stores in the country.

Our team of certified pharmacists, healthcare professionals, and logistics experts work together to ensure you receive the right medicine, at the right time, every time.
