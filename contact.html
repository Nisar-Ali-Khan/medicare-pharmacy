FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I upload a prescription?

After placing your order, you can upload the prescription through your order confirmation page or email it to us at rx@medicare.pk

What is your delivery time?

Same-day delivery in Islamabad & Rawalpindi. 2–3 business days for other cities across Pakistan.

Can I return medicines?

Returns are accepted within 24 hours for damaged or incorrect items. Prescription medicines cannot be returned once dispensed.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept Cash on Delivery, Easypaisa, JazzCash, bank transfer, and all major credit/debit cards.
